JPMorganChase is rolling out Claude across the firm, to more employees beyond engineers. In the Commercial and Investment Bank, work moves into production faster, and the firm redeploys that capacity against its business priorities.

Teams are modernizing other legacy systems, and engineers are building their own agents, including pull request review and self-healing agents, and sharing what works.

Infrastructure and operations teams use Claude Code (opens in new tab) for monitoring, alerting and root cause analysis. The firm's security team uses our frontier models to find and fix vulnerabilities faster, work we first described in Project Glasswing (opens in new tab).

## Reaching product teams and advisors

The people closest to the business are building with Claude too. Product teams turn requirements directly into engineering backlogs, and other teams use Claude Code for risk and controls assessments and program planning.

Knowledge work is already underway. Much of the product development lifecycle happens outside the code: research, requirements, design and planning.

In their Asset & Wealth Management business, JPMorganChase is putting the people with the deepest business insight and the boldest ideas alongside their technology and product experts into small, empowered teams. This is helping them to more easily build and deliver customized client investment solutions at scale. Claude Code is now part of their engineering workflow, and teams are moving faster while putting every change through more rigorous testing before it reaches clients.

Last month, J.P. Morgan Asset Management announced a connector that brings 55ip's ActiveTax Technology® into Claude for Financial Advisors (opens in new tab), putting tax-aware portfolio transition analysis into advisors' daily work.

## Built for a regulated firm

Claude Code runs in controlled environments, including Devspace, the firm’s containerized development platform, on its own infrastructure. The intent is to prevent agents from getting direct access to employee credentials or internal systems. Access widens in stages, and new Claude models go through the firm’s own approval process before anyone there can use them.

Code ships under the same discipline. Thousands of automated tests and approval gates check every change, and an automated gate returns a hard pass or halt that the pipeline enforces, so Claude can't sign off on its own work. People stay accountable for every production change.

Over the past year, JPMorganChase’s use of Claude has grown from software engineering to cyber defense to tools for financial advisors, and what JPMorganChase asks of us is shaping what we build for other regulated institutions. Claude working inside the controls of one of the world’s largest financial institutions is what we are building toward.
