We've seen Claude Code change how developers work. Engineers went from manually coding line by line, to describing ideas and reviewing what Claude built. It reconnected them with the joy of building. Our goal is to bring that magic to every team, and today we're expanding what Claude makes possible. We're introducing new artifact types with Claude Sheets, Claude Whiteboards, and Claude Data. We're also making it easier to work in your most-used apps with an upgrade to interactive connectors. Claude Meetings is coming soon.

## All your connectors and context

In Claude Code, the context Claude needs is already in the codebase. For other teams, context sprawls across emails, apps, and drives. Claude reaches it through connectors built on the Model Context Protocol (opens in new tab) (MCP), the open standard we introduced for connecting AI to the tools you use. Claude Marketplace lists more than 9,000 connectors and plugins, including community-built ones.

Interactive connectors (opens in new tab) take this further. Starting today, partners can embed their apps directly in Claude, so you can work in Adobe, Atlassian, and others without switching tabs. Plugins from Salesforce (beta), NetSuite, and Workday (beta) come with built-in skills, so sellers can prep for a call, accountants can analyze financial data, and HR teams can handle time off and expenses.

## Bring ideas to life faster, together

Starting today, Claude can build spreadsheets, whiteboards, and dashboards with your team. These work with its existing abilities to create documents, slide decks, and designs (opens in new tab), and with Claude Motion (opens in new tab) (beta), which helps you turn content into short animations. In each one, Claude quickly picks up what you need, so your thinking sets the pace of work:

- New artifact types (opens in new tab):

- In Claude Sheets, your team and Claude build spreadsheets together, collaboratively organize data, create and edit formulas, and work across multiple tabs.
- Claude Whiteboards is a digital whiteboard that your team can use to lay out and organize ideas together in a meeting, then ask Claude to summarize the findings.
- Claude Data (formerly Dashboards) connects to tools like Snowflake and Salesforce and can use definitions your data team certifies (beta) to create dashboards, visual data stories, or narrated short videos.

You can also ask Claude to build custom artifacts for the way your team works, like an account tracker, a daily briefing, a project status board, or a contract review table. Since Claude carries the same context through every step, an idea can carry over from a whiteboard to a spreadsheet to a dashboard, without the need to reiterate the details.

Coming soon, Claude Meetings will let you add Claude to a call to take notes and update the documents your team is working on during the conversation.

## Built for and with enterprises

As Claude starts to connect to more tools, businesses need better ways to oversee its access and actions. We build in security and compliance controls to help enterprises meet their own requirements, and we use the same Claude for our own most sensitive work. On Enterprise plans, admins decide who can use which products and connectors, and set spend limits for the organization at a granular level.

That control applies to the tools Claude connects to. With Claude auto-connect (opens in new tab), now in beta on eligible Team and Enterprise plans, connecting an app like Notion, Atlassian, or Dropbox is a one-time setup. Employees are then connected to the tool the first time they use it in Claude, instead of connecting each one individually.

Whichever way a tool is connected, Claude works within your existing permissions. Admins can also limit what each connector does, meaning that Claude might be able to read Slack but not send messages on your behalf.

Protecting sensitive business data is core to how we build, and we're continuing to harden those protections as Claude takes on longer, more complex work:

- Data from Enterprise organizations (opens in new tab) is encrypted in transit and at rest.
- On Enterprise plans, security teams can see how Claude is being used (opens in new tab), through audit logs and a Compliance API that works with more than 100 security and compliance tools, including Splunk and CrowdStrike.
- In the new Claude experience on Enterprise, each cloud session runs in its own isolated virtual machine (opens in new tab), with connector credentials kept outside it.

## Work that keeps up with your ideas

Modern work is set up around software, but Claude works around you. It knows your team's work through the tools you connect, helps you bring ideas to life in the formats you need, and runs within the controls your admins set. That leaves you more time to focus and make progress on big goals.
